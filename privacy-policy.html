1. No Data Collection

Data Firewall does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app operates 100% offline on your device. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers, emails, phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Browsing history or app usage sent to servers
  • Device IDs for tracking

2. How The App Works

The app uses Android VpnService API to create a LOCAL VPN (loopback, 127.0.0.1). This is a firewall technique that does NOT send your data to any external server. All blocking and filtering happens locally on your phone. Internet traffic never leaves your device through our servers because we have no servers.

Usage statistics (TrafficStats, UsageStatsManager) are read locally and displayed only to you.

3. Permissions Explained

  • VPNService (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): To block internet per app locally
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: To show data usage per app
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To auto-start protection after reboot (user enabled)
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: To list installed apps for firewall control
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To keep firewall running

No permission is used to collect personal data.

4. Third Parties & Ads

This app contains ZERO ads, ZERO trackers, ZERO analytics SDKs (No AdMob, No Firebase Analytics, No Facebook SDK). Your data is never shared with third parties because it is never collected.

5. Children's Privacy

App is safe for all ages. No data collection from children.
